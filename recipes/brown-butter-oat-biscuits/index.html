Brown Butter Oat Biscuits

Chewy oat biscuits with a toasty, nutty flavour from browned butter. Makes about 20.

Ingredients

  • 125g unsalted butter
  • ½ cup (100g) brown sugar, firmly packed
  • ¼ cup (55g) caster sugar
  • 1 egg, at room temperature
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (150g) plain flour
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1½ cups (135g) rolled oats

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a light-coloured saucepan over medium heat. Keep cooking, swirling often, until it smells nutty and the solids turn golden brown, about 5 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and cool for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and line two baking trays with baking paper.
  3. Whisk the sugars into the cooled butter, then whisk in the egg and vanilla.
  4. Stir in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt, then the oats.
  5. Roll level tablespoons of dough into balls and place them 5cm apart on the trays. Flatten each one slightly.
  6. Bake for 11–13 minutes, until golden at the edges but still soft in the middle.
  7. Cool on the trays for 5 minutes, then move to a wire rack.

Tips

  • If the dough feels too soft to roll, chill it for 30 minutes.
  • Use rolled oats, not quick oats, for a chewier biscuit.
  • Store in an airtight container for up to a week.
