No-Bake Lemon Coconut Slice

A no-bake slice with a lemony biscuit and coconut base and a tangy lemon icing. It makes 18 squares and needs about 2 hours in the fridge.

Recipe page with JSON-LD structured data and a Notes section.

Ingredients

Base

  • 250g plain sweet biscuits (such as Marie or arrowroot), finely crushed
  • 1 cup (85g) desiccated coconut, plus extra to sprinkle
  • Finely grated zest of 1 lemon
  • 125g unsalted butter, chopped
  • 200g sweetened condensed milk (about half a 395g can)

Lemon icing

  • 1½ cups (240g) icing sugar, sifted
  • 20g unsalted butter, softened
  • 2–3 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Line an 18 x 28cm slice pan with baking paper, leaving some overhanging on the long sides.
  2. Crush the biscuits to fine crumbs in a food processor, or in a sealed bag with a rolling pin.
  3. Mix the crumbs, coconut and lemon zest in a large bowl.
  4. Melt the butter and condensed milk together in a small saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  5. Pour the butter mixture into the dry ingredients and mix until evenly combined. Press firmly and evenly into the pan.
  6. Refrigerate for 1 hour, until firm.
  7. For the icing, beat the icing sugar, softened butter and 2 tablespoons of lemon juice until smooth, adding more juice a little at a time if it's too stiff to spread.
  8. Spread the icing over the base, sprinkle with extra coconut and refrigerate for another hour before cutting into squares.

Notes

  • Condensed milk: use full-fat sweetened condensed milk. It's what binds the base, so light versions make it crumbly.
  • Dairy-free: I've had good results with coconut condensed milk and a firm dairy-free baking block in place of the butter.
  • Gluten-free: swap in gluten-free plain sweet biscuits, and check the packet, as some contain wheat starch.
  • Storage: keeps for up to a week in an airtight container in the fridge.
