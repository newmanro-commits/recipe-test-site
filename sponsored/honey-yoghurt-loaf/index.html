Honey Yoghurt Loaf

Sponsored-style recipe page for testing URL exclusions (exclude /recipe-test-site/sponsored/*).

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (225g) self-raising flour
  • ½ cup (110g) caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ cup (200g) Greek yoghurt
  • ⅓ cup (80ml) vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup (90g) honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C (150°C fan). Grease and line a 22 x 10cm loaf tin.
  2. Whisk the eggs, yoghurt, oil, honey and vanilla in a large bowl until smooth.
  3. Add the flour and sugar and fold until just combined.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake for 50–55 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  5. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
